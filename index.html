FAQs

Questions about our CAD workflow?

What does CrackDent provide?

CrackDent provides digital dental CAD design support for dental laboratories and dental professionals. We focus on design and digital file delivery, not manufacturing or clinical treatment.

Which file formats can I submit?

Share the relevant case files and prescription. STL, DICOM and supporting case notes or reference images may be used depending on the case requirements.

Which file formats are delivered?

Common delivery formats include STL and ZIP packages, based on the case requirements.

Can I request revisions?

Yes. The workflow includes client review so you can provide specific adjustment notes before final delivery.

Does CrackDent manufacture dental products?

No. CrackDent provides digital CAD design only. Manufacturing, clinical treatment and production remain with your dental workflow.
