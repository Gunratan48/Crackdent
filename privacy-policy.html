Your Privacy Matters

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

1. About this Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Crackdent ("we", "us" or "our") collects and uses information when you visit this website or submit an enquiry. For privacy questions, contact Support@crackdent.com.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our enquiry form, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, selected service, message, consent choice and technical submission metadata. Do not submit patient-identifiable records, medical histories, scans containing personal identifiers or sensitive health information through the general enquiry form.

3. Purpose

We use enquiry details to respond to your request, communicate about the service selected, provide support, prevent spam or misuse, maintain necessary business records and meet legal obligations.

4. Consent and Choice

The enquiry form requires an affirmative privacy consent checkbox before submission. You may choose not to submit the form. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent by contacting us; this does not affect processing already carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

5. Email Service Provider

Enquiry information submitted through this form is transmitted to our server-side function and used to send an email notification through Brevo (Sendinblue SAS) to Support@crackdent.com. The email provider may process the information on infrastructure operated by it and its service providers. Before launch, the website operator must review the provider's current data processing terms, processing locations, transfer arrangements and security settings.

6. Retention and Security

We retain enquiry information only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain business records, resolve disputes and meet legal requirements. Access should be restricted to authorised personnel. Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards should be maintained, including secure API-key storage and account access controls. Email copies may remain in the recipient mailbox according to its retention settings.

7. Your Requests and Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of or erasure of your personal information, withdraw consent or raise a privacy grievance. Contact Support@crackdent.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We may verify your identity before acting and will respond within applicable legal timelines.

8. Children

This general business enquiry form is not directed at children. Do not submit a child's personal information through it.

9. Cookies and Analytics

This starter website does not intentionally include advertising or analytics trackers. If analytics, advertising pixels, chat widgets, embedded media or non-essential cookies are added later, this policy and any required consent controls must be updated before activation.

10. Updates

We may update this Policy when our services, technology or legal obligations change. The latest version and update date will be published on this page.
